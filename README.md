## 🌟 Overview

Scents & Petals is a conceptual UX/UI design project focused on transforming a traditional perfume and flower shop into a seamless, elegant, and efficient online experience. The goal is to eliminate the inconvenience of physical shopping and create a digital platform that offers:

- Convenience  
- Premium product quality  
- Personalization  
- Reliable delivery  
- A smooth, intuitive user experience  

This case study documents the foundational UX process behind the concept, including research, personas, information architecture, user flows, and early design direction.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Customers want to buy flowers and perfumes without the inconvenience of visiting a physical shop. They need:

- Convenience  
- A wider and more curated selection  
- Clear, trustworthy product information  
- Reliable and discreet delivery  
- A smooth, intuitive online shopping experience  

Scents & Petals aims to solve these needs while establishing a strong, recognizable, and trustworthy digital brand.

## 🧩 Business Goals

1. **Enable easy online ordering**  
   Allow customers to browse, select, and purchase products without needing to visit a physical shop.

2. **Increase sales and reach**  
   Expand beyond local, walk‑in customers and reach a wider online audience.

3. **Showcase product variety**  
   Present a clear, visually appealing range of flowers and perfumes.

4. **Provide transparent product information**  
   Offer detailed descriptions, pricing, delivery details, and customization options.

5. **Build strong brand recognition**  
   Establish Scents & Petals as a reputable, convenient, and premium online store.

## 📦 Project Scope (Phase 1)

The initial phase of Scents & Petals focuses on building a functional, user‑friendly MVP with the following core features:

- **Product Listings**  
  Organized categories for flowers and perfumes.

- **Detailed Product Pages**  
  High‑quality images, descriptions, pricing, scent notes, flower types, and customization options.

- **Shopping Cart**  
  Add, remove, and review selected items.

- **Secure Checkout**  
  Smooth, trusted payment flow with delivery scheduling.

- **Contact Page**  
  Easy access for inquiries or custom requests.

- **Delivery Options**  
  Clear delivery windows, fees, and instructions.

- **User Accounts (Optional)**  
  Saved addresses, order history, and re‑ordering for returning customers.

- **Blog / Tips Section (Future Enhancement)**  
  Flower care, perfume layering, and gifting guides.

## 🔍 User Research

To understand the needs, motivations, and frustrations of potential customers, I conducted foundational UX research focusing on real-world shopping behaviors and expectations.

### **Target Customer Groups**
- Gift buyers (birthdays, anniversaries, holidays)  
- Self‑purchasers  
- Corporate clients  
- Mixed age groups with varying tech familiarity  

### **Research Methods**
- **Interviews** — to understand emotional motivations and pain points  
- **Surveys** — to gather broader behavioral insights  
- **Competitor Analysis** — to identify gaps in existing online flower/perfume stores  
- **Online Review Analysis** — to uncover recurring complaints and unmet needs  

These insights shaped the personas, information architecture, and user flows that follow.

## 🔄 User Flows

Each persona follows a unique journey shaped by their motivations, expectations, and context. These flows guided the structure of the site and informed key interaction decisions.

---

### **Dorian — Fast, Premium, Frictionless Ordering**

1. Lands on homepage  
2. Navigates to **Birthday** or **Luxury Flowers**  
3. Evaluates premium arrangements  
4. Customizes bouquet (size, vase, message)  
5. Selects delivery window  
6. Completes fast, secure checkout  
7. Receives instant confirmation + tracking  

---

### **Julian — Detailed Evaluation & Informed Decision-Making**

1. Searches for **Anniversary Flowers** or **New Perfumes**  
2. Reads scent notes, flower types, dimensions, and reviews  
3. Compares multiple products  
4. Adds selected items to cart  
5. Schedules delivery  
6. Adds personalized message

### **Genesis — Corporate-Grade Sourcing & Subscription Management**

1. Searches **Corporate / Office Flowers**

These flows ensure Scents & Petals supports both emotional gift‑givers and high‑precision professional users.

## 🧪 Design & Prototyping

The design process for Scents & Petals followed a structured UX approach, moving from low‑fidelity exploration to more refined visual concepts.

### **Wireframing (Low‑Fidelity)**
Early sketches and grayscale layouts were created to define:
- Homepage structure  
- Navigation hierarchy  
- Product listing layouts  
- Product detail pages  
- Cart and checkout flow  

These wireframes focused on usability, clarity, and reducing cognitive load.

---

### **Prototyping (Mid‑ to High‑Fidelity)**
Interactive prototypes were developed to visualize user flows and test interactions:
- Mid‑fidelity grayscale screens  
- Clickable prototypes in Figma  
- Early visual hierarchy and spacing decisions  
- Iterative refinements based on usability insights  

---

### **UI Design**
The visual direction emphasizes elegance, freshness, and premium quality through:
- A refined color palette inspired by florals and luxury fragrances  
- Clean, modern typography  
- Soft shadows and minimalistic components  
- Early design system elements (buttons, cards, inputs, icons)
