# 🌸 Orna Blooms & Fragrances — UX Case Study

*A UX/UI case study focused on designing a trustworthy, elegant, and user-centered online experience for purchasing perfumes and flowers.*

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Orna Blooms & Fragrances is a conceptual e-commerce UX/UI project exploring how thoughtful digital experiences can improve the process of purchasing perfumes and flowers online.

The project was created as part of my independent UX learning journey inspired by the Google UX Design Certificate process, with a strong focus on:
- User research
- Empathy-driven design
- Information architecture
- User flows
- Low-fidelity wireframing
- Interaction design

This repository documents the UX process from research to early prototyping.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many users enjoy buying perfumes and flowers, but the online purchasing experience often feels unreliable, confusing, or impersonal.

Common frustrations discovered during research included:
- Not being able to test perfumes before purchasing
- Lack of trustworthy reviews
- Poor product descriptions
- Delivery delays
- Flowers arriving damaged or wilted
- Hidden costs at checkout
- Difficulty trusting unfamiliar online stores

The challenge for this project is:

> How might we design an online perfume and flower shopping experience that feels trustworthy, convenient, elegant, and emotionally satisfying for users?

## 🔍 Research Process

The project began with foundational UX research to better understand how people currently purchase perfumes and flowers, what frustrates them, and what would increase trust in online shopping experiences.

### Research Methods
- User interviews
- Google Forms survey
- Competitive analysis
- Personal experience analysis
- Observation of existing perfume retailers on social media

---

## 📊 Key Research Findings

### Perfume Shopping Insights
Users expressed concerns about:
- Buying perfumes without smelling them first
- Receiving scents that do not match expectations
- Too many choices without guidance
- Lack of trustworthy reviews

Research also revealed:
- Users heavily rely on recommendations from friends
- Positive reviews increase trust significantly
- Most users prefer buying full bottles instead of refill systems
- Users showed strong interest in perfume discovery subscriptions

---

### Flower Shopping Insights
Users valued:
- Fresh flowers
- Reliable delivery
- Affordable pricing
- Ability to customize arrangements

Major frustrations included:
- Wilted flowers
- Poor packaging
- Hidden fees
- Limited flower variety
- Late deliveries
- Lack of clear online descriptions

---
